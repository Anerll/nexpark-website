About Nexpark Enterprises Limited

A Zambian-owned multi-sector enterprise built on trust, quality and excellence since 2023.

A Zambian Enterprise Built on Trust & Excellence

Nexpark Enterprises Limited is a Zambian-owned enterprise established in 2023 with a mission to deliver exceptional business services and products to both public and private sectors. Our company is committed to providing tailored solutions that cater to the unique needs of different sectors, including government agencies and private enterprises.

Nexpark offers a wide range of services from importation and sale of motor vehicles, car hire services, agriculture, to building & construction and supply of various products.

Our comprehensive range of products and services caters to a diverse clientele, ensuring that whether you're a business owner, contractor, or individual seeking quality solutions, you'll find precisely what you're looking for.
